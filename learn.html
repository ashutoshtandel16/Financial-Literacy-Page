Build your understanding of everyday financial concepts.

Digital payments allow people to transfer money electronically without using physical cash.

  • UPI
  • QR payments
  • Debit and credit cards
  • Digital wallets

Banks provide services such as saving, transferring and borrowing money.

  • Savings accounts
  • Interest
  • Loans
  • Fixed deposits

Saving means setting aside part of your income for future needs and goals.

  • Emergency funds
  • Monthly budgeting
  • 50/30/20 rule

Investing involves putting money into assets with the expectation of earning a return.

  • Stocks
  • Mutual funds
  • SIP
  • Risk and return

Never share sensitive banking information with unknown people or websites.

  • Never share OTPs
  • Never share your UPI PIN
  • Avoid suspicious links
  • Use official banking applications
